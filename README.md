# Presell notes

Working notes on presell pages, advertorials and campaign economics, written by the ClickBanger editorial team. ClickBanger builds presell pages for DTC brands, so the arithmetic of paid traffic comes up often.

## Break-even ROAS before any benchmark

Return on ad spend divides revenue by ad spend, so the same 2.5x can pay a whole team for one store and lose money for the next. The deciding number is the margin left on each order.

A store that keeps 40% of each sale after product cost, shipping, payment fees and returns needs 2.50 of revenue per 1.00 of ads to break even. A store that keeps 25% needs 4.00. Both can report a 2.5x ROAS and only one is making money.

The rule is 1 divided by the margin rate. The gap between that floor and your target is the room you actually have for testing creatives, audiences and presell pages.

To run the arithmetic on your own numbers, including CPA and AOV, see the [ROAS and break-even calculator](https://clickbanger.com/tools/roas-calculator).

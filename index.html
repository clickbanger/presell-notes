Break-even ROAS: the number to check before any benchmark

Return on ad spend divides revenue by ad spend, so the same 2.5x figure can pay a whole team for one store and lose money for the next. What decides it is the margin left on each order, not the average of the market.

Take a store that keeps 40% of each sale after product cost, shipping, payment fees and returns. Every 1.00 of revenue keeps 0.40, so it needs 2.50 of revenue per 1.00 of ads just to break even. A store that keeps 25% needs 4.00. Both can report a 2.5x ROAS and only one of them is making money.

The calculation is therefore 1 divided by the margin rate. Once you have that floor, the distance between it and your target is the room you actually have for testing new creatives, audiences and presell pages.
